Michael Weekly Invoices

Private desktop application · Owner use only

This application helps its owner prepare weekly measurement invoices from a reviewed Excel workbook. It runs on the owner's Windows computer.

The invoicing workflow

  1. Prepare and review the weekly measurement workbook.
  2. Create one QuickBooks Online invoice line for the reviewed weekly total, with quantity 1 and no tax.
  3. Attach the detailed PDF customer and job breakdown.
  4. Review the invoice in QuickBooks Online and send it there, with the owner's copy address.

The integration is being validated. Production use remains dependent on Intuit access and verification of the complete workflow. These pages do not create, change, or send invoices.

There is no public sign-up, payment processing, or public access to accounting data. This application is not offered for sale.
