Privacy policy

Effective October 11, 2026. This policy describes the private desktop application Michael Weekly Invoices and these informational pages.

Information used by the desktop application

The application uses the owner's measurement workbook and PDF breakdown, including customer and job names, project identifiers, scheduled dates, installation addresses, job links, quantities and billing amounts. It reads QuickBooks company, customer, product, payment-term and invoice information needed to prepare and verify the owner's invoices.

Purpose and transfer

Information is used to calculate and verify weekly billing, prepare invoices, attach the job breakdown, and prevent duplicate weekly invoices. The owner authorizes the connection to Intuit QuickBooks Online. Invoice information and the selected PDF are uploaded to Intuit for this purpose. Invoice recipients receive invoice information and attachments when the owner sends the invoice through QuickBooks.

The application does not sell data, run advertising, or provide a public API for accounting data. It contains no generative AI feature. The owner may separately use assistance or services to develop, maintain, or troubleshoot the application; those services are outside its automatic invoice processing.

Storage and protection

OAuth credentials are encrypted for the current Windows user using Windows DPAPI and stored in Local AppData, outside the owner's synced invoice folders. Credentials are not embedded in source code or published on this website. API traffic to Intuit uses HTTPS. Workbooks, PDFs, local verification records and duplicate-prevention records are retained for the owner's invoicing work. Existing invoice folders may sync through the owner's OneDrive or Dropbox accounts and are governed by those services and the owner's account settings.

Control and retention

The owner controls the connected QuickBooks company, the local files, the synced copies, and the recipients. The owner can disconnect the app in QuickBooks, stop running it, and remove its local credentials and files. Disconnecting does not delete invoices or attachments already in QuickBooks, email already sent, or cloud backup copies. Those records can be managed in the respective accounts, subject to applicable recordkeeping needs.

This informational website

These static pages collect no invoice information, credentials, uploads or form submissions and use no application analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the pages and may record visitor IP addresses and other service information under the GitHub privacy statement.

Questions and changes

This is an owner-operated private tool. Questions or corrections should be directed to the owner through the existing business contact. The effective date above will change when this policy is updated.
