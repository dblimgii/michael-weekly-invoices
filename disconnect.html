Disconnect QuickBooks

To stop the integration, stop running the desktop tool and disconnect Michael Weekly Invoices in your QuickBooks Online connected-app settings.

If you arrived here after disconnecting in QuickBooks, keep the tool stopped. Reauthorization is required before API operations can resume.

The owner can also remove the local encrypted credential file from the application's Local AppData folder on the Lenovo. Existing invoices, attachments, emails and local or synced invoice files are separate business records; disconnecting does not delete them.
