Michael Weekly Invoices runs on the owner's Lenovo Windows computer. This website contains setup information; it does not run the invoice processor.

During setup, continue using the existing weekly draft and final PDF shortcuts. The QuickBooks bridge remains a separate validation tool until production access and the full review/send workflow are verified.

To check a connection, use the local QuickBooks connection tool on the Lenovo. To establish or renew authorization, follow the connection instructions.

Once invoicing is enabled, review the resulting invoice and attached PDF in QuickBooks Online before choosing Send.
